## Original 48 V Outpost network and losses

Canonical source: `src/physics/power-ledger-t02.js` and `src/physics/outposts.js`. The original `StarfallOutpostUI.store` owns solar arrays, physical wire endpoints, individual battery `energyJ`, heat, load switches and preserved laboratory receipts.

- Nominal DC bus **48 V**; modeled current limit **500 A**. Node load acceptance is decided by original priority/supply solver; unrelated nearby objects are **not** electrically connected unless the original wire tool joins their asset IDs.
- Source electrical power from actual solar angle, local star irradiance, atmosphere, occlusion, weather, real array efficiency and condition. Solar is *not* a permanently generating asset; night/eclipses can produce zero.
- Connected-battery discharge has **93%** bus efficiency (`ETA_DISCHARGE = 0.93`); incoming charge stores **92%** of permitted input joules (`ETA_CHARGE = 0.92`). A wired charging transfer also accounts for `WIRE_LOSS = 0.015`; the source reports actual `wireLossW`, `chargedJ`, `dischargedJ` and `lossJ`.
- Original transfer `T02.transfer`: energy drawn is bounded by source actual joules, power × duration, source reserve, target headroom and requested joules/efficiency. **`deliveredJ = drawnJ × efficiency`, `lossJ = drawnJ − deliveredJ`**. Thus no ordinary transfer creates net energy; all energy must be debited from the source owner.
- T13 ship↔rover umbilical: original ship **2 MJ safety reserve**, **6 kW** transfer for at most **40 s** at a time, actual T02 energy debits, finite efficiency, connector temperature, manual latch/undock, original T02 power bus and T13 source receipts. This cannot be invoked remotely without the original in-world physical target.
- Rover T12: battery charging, winch and physical field repairs debit the original truck, Outpost, vehicle or compatible source, and remain subject to distance, cable, weather and finite thermal/time requirements.
- Planetary solar, atmosphere and timed rotation mean a day/night output value is specific to **body, location, exact simulation time** and cannot be predeclared. Verify `StarfallOutpostUI.powerReports.get(bodyId)` for actual measured model results.

## Radio traffic, exactly-once custody and physical limitations

Authoritative source: `src/physics/mission-transport-t03.js`, `mission-comms-ui-t03.js`, T04 and T08 real powered station routing.

| Item | Source-derived bound / meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Radio packet archive | `MAX_PACKETS=240`; `MAX_BYTES=12,000,000`. T17 preserves legitimate older saved packets, but **new** admission refuses at the cap. |
| Integration | **5 s** deterministic absolute frame, at most **12** steps on a call, physical simulation clock; long jumps enter awaiting review rather than teleporting data. |
| RF rate | Source receiver/link supplies `bandwidthBps`, power, line of sight, hops. Bytes per step are `floor(minimum effective link bit rate × seconds / 8)`, further bounded by packet size and route conditions. |
| RF energy | For transmitted bytes, source debit is **`txPowerW × sentBytes × 8 / bandwidthBps` joules**. If the original power owner refuses the debit, transfer pauses. |
| Receipt | T03 `ACKED` is the final durable success; `CREATED`, `WAITING_FOR_ROUTE`, `TRANSMITTING`, `IN_FLIGHT`, `RECEIVED`, `PAUSED` or missing route are **not** a completed remote operation. Never rename numeric 7C sensor values as rendered 7F images. |
| Blackout | Packet bytes and onboard observations wait for contact; a received command executes only after the actual rover receiver has power and ACK processing. No fake phone-home callback from a disconnected world. |
| Long-distance | Cross-world T08 route requires commissioned, electrically connected, powered counterpart RF infrastructure; **FICTIONAL** interstellar carrier mode is explicitly labelled fictional, not real known RF physics. |
| Ship transceiver option | Original 45 scene-unit / **128 kbit/s** default radio; optional T15 module reaches 85 scene units / **192 kbit/s** with higher **1,450 W** avionics load and **95 W** modeled transmit power. Both use original T03 source battery and LOS checks. |
| Cameras | Real 7F WebGL2 encoded binary, thumbnail and IndexedDB original file ID. Only after actual radio receipt/ACK is a downlinked photo inspectable; T17 reports MISSING IMAGE FILE when metadata exists but binary is lost. |

The earlier **T08 Chromium model integration** proves a four-node cross-body radio route, controlled predeployed rover, real software-WebGL off-world image and finite ACK after deliberate blackout; see `tests/interplanetary-t08-browser.py`. It **does not prove** that the player built both planetary bases and flew/injected that rover or that the entire chain was operated exclusively by onscreen UI. ## Source custody IDs / anti-duplication

Each autonomous rover numeric `SAMPLE` has a unique original observation ID and body-fixed coordinates, stored in its onboard records before potential T03 downlink. The original Science Journal and T10 lab retain that ID; reading it twice must not create two scientific records. Every real 7F photo has separate metadata ID and actual physical IndexedDB bytes, and a T03 photo transmission packet linked to the photo ID. T15 service events preserve before/after original component IDs and 7E run/photo/observation provenance; they never grant a replacement part by remote magic.
