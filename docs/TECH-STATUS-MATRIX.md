## Source-backed inventory and status rules

The project has **87 distinct inventoried contract entries**: 8 constructible; 32 vehicle-component; 8 scientific-instrument; 3 rocket-engine; 3 launch-payload; 3 satellite-payload; 11 abstract-contract; 19 operational-subsystem. The **11 abstract contracts** are types/interfaces, not 11 extra placed devices. Vehicle parts include defaults and selectable alternatives; a catalog entry is not automatically an installed object.

**Status levels:** `SOURCE PRESENT` = code-backed catalog/UI/model path found; `PARTIAL` = logic exists but full lifecycle is missing; `NOT VERIFIED` = no complete end-to-end execution evidence; `NOT APPLICABLE` = type-only descriptor. All T00 integration/manual columns are `NOT VERIFIED`.

## Mandatory operational acceptance contracts

These are **future proof requirements**, not tests already passed. For every device, a real in-game user must be able to **place/install → supply physical power (if active) → command/operate → observe real effect and telemetry → disconnect or trigger a safe fault → recover → save/reload**. Data systems additionally require ID/provenance, finite bytes/records and exactly-once receipt.

## Critical blockers, in dependency order

1. **T01 remote simulation:** `launch-ui.js`, `satellites-ui7c.js` and `imaging-ui7f.js` depend on loaded body/game context for important operations. Distant jobs do not share an authoritative persistent independent scheduler.
2. **T02 electricity:** Outpost group power, ship/rover power, truck battery and satellite energy each have separate accounting; generators in `vehicles.js` are `fuelIndependent` (must be labeled fictional until a consumable model exists).
3. **T03 mission transport:** `satellites7c.js::downlink` calculates future delivery after one contact check; `ground-operations7d.js::downlink` directly releases up to four onboard readings on a link; `imaging7f.js` has a separate byte-progress queue. These are **not** one dependable packet/ACK service.
4. **T04/T08 distant radio:** `launch-ui.js::remoteLink` refuses a player who is not landed on the probe's body. There is no verified Planet A → Planet B powered ground-station packet route, even if B has an orbital relay.
5. **T05 remote imagery:** `imaging-ui7f.js::poseFor` refuses an unloaded target body. Do not replace actual world pixels with artwork or current-camera screenshots.
6. **T06/T11 payload destination:** `launch-systems.js::deploy` creates the rover using the originating foundation's `m.local`; a target-site landing and descent check are absent.
7. **T09/T10 structures:** `hab` is primarily a 680 W structure; `storage` describes record capacity but not physical samples; lab records observations without the complete powered processing/photo custody path.
8. **T17 history integrity:** `outposts.js::restore` slices saved structures/wires/relays/receipts and `addReceipt` shifts older receipts. Other histories in 7A/7C/field journal also have bounded `.slice/.shift` behavior. Preserving full old saves must be explicitly proven, not presumed.
9. **T18 user mission verification:** Script/unit tests and software-WebGL screenshots are narrower than actual long-play Chromium GPU and browser-close/reopen verification.
