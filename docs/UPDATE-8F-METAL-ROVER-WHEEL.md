# STARFALL 8F: metal rover wheel (experimental)

Nine finite manufacturing jobs, requiring real installed foundry caster, lathe, grinder, manual press, and ship kit fixture. Refined and owned materials: **42.5 kg steel, 4.5 kg aluminum, 0.8 kg copper, 0.2 kg carbon** = 48 kg.

The preexisting six jobs form chassis rail, rim, hub, axle shaft, spring blank, and 7.3 kg rigid wheel core. The three new jobs form **0.92 kg copper-carbon wheel bushing** from 0.8 kg copper +0.2 kg carbon (0.08 kg scrap), **1.52 kg steel grouser/traction cleats** from 1.6 kg steel (0.08 kg scrap), and finally **10.02 kg low-speed metal wheel** from 7.3 kg core +0.92 kg bushing +1.52 kg cleats +0.4 kg steel (0.12 kg scrap).

Every operation uses actual certified inventory and meters ship electrical work and elapsed time. All material, including scrap, remains in authoritative 8A cargo lots. The complete nine-job integration test proves 48 kg conservation, stage dependency and required tools.

**Limitations:** copper-carbon plain bearing is simplified, not a verified sintered graphite bronze process. A rigid metal wheel is not a tested rover wheel with real suspension, braking, rolling dynamics, or approved axle bearings. No new truck is spawned or commissioned. 8G remains incomplete. This PR is stacked on #56 and #55, and not merged into main.
