# STARFALL Update 8G — finite modeled planetary nitrogen capture

## Implemented vertical slice
- **World composition:** Use existing CW01 atmospheric mole fractions on a pre-existing, landable, non-vacuum planet. CW01 fractions are seeded high-uncertainty **modeling priors**, NOT real spectrometer measurements or known atmospheric pressure.
- **Finite capture source:** Every immutable 8C planet-fixed surface cell has a modeled *accessible local air budget* of 2,400 kg. This number is a conservative gameplay extraction quota, not total world-atmosphere mass. The budget is not an infinite player inventory. Each extraction saves a cell-specific depletion receipt; changing machines inside one cell does not reset stock. Airless bodies, moons, stars and non-landable gas giants are refused.
- **True manufactured facilities:** Use 8F machine-kit fabrication to make a 740 kg `plant_kit_air_fractionation` from finite steel, aluminum, copper, glass, refractories, wire, motors and real control boards, then install it through existing 8E erection (1.35 MJ). Manufacture and install a separate 195 kg, 8 MPa `vessel_kit_nitrogen`. Their physical 8A asset identities and paid installation are compulsory.
- **Extraction transaction:** An EVA operator must be within 38 game scene units of the air plant, a powered landed ship within 75, and the N₂ pressure vessel within 65. The plant needs its intake, dust filter, gas separator, compressor and nitrogen-storage interface. Each **20 kg air** batch consumes **810 kJ** of the ship battery, produces **20 kg × modeled N₂ mass fraction × 0.80 efficiency** of pure nitrogen and explicitly vents the **remaining air mass**, including separation loss. This is a simplified recoverable nitrogen fraction, not an ideal industrial cryogenic design.
- **Physical destination:** The product is **not teleported to ship cargo**. It becomes a grade 0.999, documented, finite 8A nitrogen gas lot inside the same-world, rated `industrial_vessel` 8A container. Volume is calculated using the ideal-gas equation at 293.15 K and 5 MPa, and vessel pressure/capacity limits apply. The vessel's physical custody remains surface-owned. The ship and original expedition truck are unchanged.
- **Save safety:** The `atmosphere8g` state and 8A gas lots are both included in the ordinary SF save. Unique operation IDs prevent replaying an intake to mint nitrogen twice. Receipt mass closure requires `air intake = recovered nitrogen + vented mass`, and 8A validation protects container capacity.
- **Verification:** Node tests exercise physical 8F kit mass, installed equipment, pure output, CW01 fraction, hard depletion, correct exhaust balance, rated vessel capacity/contamination, EVA power, orbital-vacuum denial, and idempotence. Real offline Chromium exercises Engineer UI, actual simulated N₂ capture, ship energy, saved depletion and original truck/rover conservation. No extraction stock is seeded by gameplay; all browser fixtures are explicitly test-only.

## Required next integrations
- **Transport of physical gas storage:** A nitrogen lot in a stationary 8E vessel cannot yet satisfy 8G habitat charge's requirement for ship-owned 8A cargo. The next slice must physically load a rated vessel or move gas into an actually rated portable ship tank, accounting for both vessel and gas mass, cargo capacity, winch work, and save/load. An unlimited `ship hold` gas transfer is forbidden.
- **Finite oxygen/water delivery:** The existing 8E chemistry can generate oxygen from finite water electrolysis into a separate rated vessel, but it also requires pressure-safe vessel transport or transfer, plus a real water source to the habitat.
- **Habitat T09 operationalization:** A charged but unqualified habitat must not inherit default tutorial tank gas, infinite scrubber, power, or an unlocked airlock. Connect finite gas/heat/CO₂/water ledgers and actual T02 power before enabling occupancy or marking it habitable.
- **Rockets:** Dry-stage physical construction and repairs exist, but finite LOX/CH₄ fueling, 7B propulsion/manifests and qualification are separate prerequisites.
- **Testing:** Keep full spacecraft, original truck custody, T06 autonomous rover and offline parity gates green. Do not mark Update 8G complete or begin 8H because a nitrogen-lot-producing demo passed.

All chemistry, phase handling, atmosphere accessibility, energy and 8G scene distances are simplified engineering gameplay, not human-rated air separation or life-support certification.
