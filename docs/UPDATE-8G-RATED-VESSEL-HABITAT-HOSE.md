# Update 8G — metered O₂/N₂ transfer from physical pressure vessels into habitats

## Actual new operation

An intact, previously fabricated `habitat_envelope` from the 8G construction pad may receive **one finite initial 25 m³ atmosphere/water charge**. The player must have:

- One manufactured or installed **O₂-rated pressure vessel**, with real oxygen gas lots inside its individually owned `vessel8e:<asset>` 8A gas container.
- One separate physically rated **N₂ pressure vessel** with real nitrogen lots. The nitrogen separation chain introduced in #78 creates these stock lots, and #80 enables carrying a filled rated vessel aboard a spacecraft.
- **20 kg of physical water** in the ship-owned 8A material hold. It is never generated by this operation.
- An original structural manufacturing receipt; an undamaged shell (>=95%); EVA within 24 game scene units of the habitat, a powered landed ship within 100 and each gas source within 95 scene units using a sealed regulator hose.
- Source vessels must be genuine enabled, intact (>=80%) 8E pressure vessels with >=5 MPa simulated pressure rating and a dedicated compatible gas container. A source may be on this planet or properly secured in the ship's `engineering-gas-cradle`, not in a magic inventory location.
- **1.5 MJ of ship engineering-battery work** for metering and gas handling.

The exact lot-level source debits are recorded by ID. Gas remains in its original vessel up to the moment of transfer: it is **not** teleported into the ship's standard material hold. The ship water lot is independently debited. Precisely the sum of physically removed oxygen, nitrogen and water mass is credited to the existing habitat physical asset and retained across save/reload.

The air amount is evaluated using the **existing T09 ideal gas law** at 293.15 K: target 21 kPa O₂ plus 78 kPa inert N₂, nominal total 99 kPa. Both partial pressures are checked. The gas phase is recorded in one new T09 state with zero magically added oxygen reserve, nitrogen reserve, carbon scrubber, filtered air, backup battery, or occupants.

**This is pressure charging, not qualification.** The pressure envelope's `habitable`, `lifeSupportOnline`, `commissioned`, `operational`, and `airlockSealed` flags remain false. Physical static gas present at T09 pressure does not prove the habitat is safe or functional; no ongoing leakage, metabolism, gas regeneration or airlock cycle is connected. Real-world safety certification is not implied.

## Anti-duplication and save constraints

- An intact existing manufacturing receipt and real same-world 8A habitat asset ID are mandatory.
- Material reserves are honored; any overdraw, unsuitable grade, source mismatch, rated-vessel contamination, missing water, insufficient pressure rating, unsafe distance or insufficient 1.5 MJ ship energy refuses the operation without mutating snapshots.
- The new one-time `meteredCharge8g` receipt is also stored in `habitatCharge8g`. That intentionally makes the earlier raw ship-stock habitat charging route reject a second initial charge, preventing two separate charge pathways from generating twice the designed gas mass.
- Changes are pure in the contract and are committed only after full 8A validation and an available battery balance. The existing expedition truck, science rover, gas-vessel identities, source lot provenance, game render and planetary bodies are never replaced.

Manufactured rockets also still require genuinely sourced propellant, tanks and tested powered 7B stages rather than simply mapping dry-stage scaffolds onto legacy unlimited flight presets. Only after the full 8G feature gates and regression tests pass should 8H interplanetary logistics be started.
